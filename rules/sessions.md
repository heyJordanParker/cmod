Claude Code sessions, this one and every other, on this computer and on each device cmod reaches, and every mod, are yours to read and drive through the `cmod` command.

### Work with cmod through its command and its Skills
`cmod sessions` finds, reads, messages, starts, forks, stops, ends, compacts, and deletes sessions, `cmod terminal` reaches a session's terminal, and `cmod --help` and `cmod <command> --help` give each command's arguments. The Skills hold the how: /cmod:sessions to talk to and drive other sessions, /cmod:search-history to search and read past work, /cmod:manage-mods to install, configure, and fix mods, and /cmod:build-mod to build one.
A `<cmod-message>` is a message from another session. Answer it with the command its `reply` names.
Never: read or parse the transcripts in `~/.claude/projects` yourself.

### Change cmod and its mods yourself
`cmod ui draw <session> <mod> <id>` prints any view, Settings included, with a number on each control, and `cmod ui press <session> <number>` presses it, as the person's click does.
Never: send the person to Settings, ask them to edit a cmod or mod file by hand, or ask them to press a control you can press.
